Exhibit№ 20

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33

2025 · Sandfall Interactive · RPG

A breakout debut that fused turn-based tactics with real-time dodges and parries, wrapped in a Belle Époque art style. One of the most acclaimed newcomers in years, from a studio that had never shipped a game before.

Exhibit№ 19

Undertale

2015 · Toby Fox · RPG

A scrappy indie RPG that let you befriend every monster instead of fighting them, then made sure you felt the full weight of every choice along the way.

Exhibit№ 18

Street Fighter II

1991 · Capcom · Fighting

The arcade cabinet that invented the modern fighting game and turned local arcades into the first real esports arenas.

Exhibit№ 17

Tetris

1984 · Alexey Pajitnov · Puzzle

Falling blocks, endless strategic depth. Four decades on, nobody has designed a more perfect puzzle.

Exhibit№ 16

Super Mario Bros.

1985 · Nintendo · Platformer

The side-scroller that helped pull the games industry back from the brink after the crash of 1983, and taught the world what a video game could be.

Exhibit№ 15

Doom

1993 · id Software · Shooter

id Software's blisteringly fast shooter didn't just launch a genre — it helped make PC gaming a mass hobby almost overnight.

Exhibit№ 14

God of War

2018 · Santa Monica Studio · Action-Adventure

Kratos traded chaotic button-mashing for a single unbroken camera and a story about fatherhood, reinventing a franchise nobody expected to go this deep.

Exhibit№ 13

Baldur's Gate 3

2023 · Larian Studios · RPG

Larian took the tabletop promise of "you can try anything" and somehow delivered on it at an almost impossible scale, reigniting an entire genre.

Exhibit№ 12

Final Fantasy VII

1997 · Square · RPG

The JRPG that turned Cloud, Sephiroth, and Midgar into household names and proved role-playing games could be a global blockbuster.

Exhibit№ 11

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

1998 · Nintendo · Action-Adventure

The blueprint for modern action-adventure design, from targeting systems to dungeon structure, that the genre is still building on today.

Exhibit№ 10

Super Mario 64

1996 · Nintendo · Platformer

Nintendo didn't just add a third dimension to platforming — they wrote the grammar for how 3D movement in games should feel.

Exhibit№ 09

Portal 2

2011 · Valve · Puzzle

A first-person puzzler about portals turned out to have one of gaming's sharpest scripts, pairing devious spatial logic with genuinely great comedy.

Exhibit№ 08

Half-Life 2

2004 · Valve · Shooter

Valve's physics-driven shooter set a bar for environmental storytelling and set-piece design that studios are still chasing two decades later.

Exhibit№ 07

Dark Souls

2011 · FromSoftware · Action RPG

FromSoftware's punishing, interconnected world taught a generation of designers that difficulty and mystery could be a language of their own.

Exhibit№ 06

Minecraft

2011 · Mojang · Sandbox

One block-building idea from an indie developer became a cultural phenomenon, handing an entire generation their first taste of unlimited creative freedom.

Exhibit№ 05

Grand Theft Auto V

2013 · Rockstar Games · Open World

Rockstar's three-protagonist crime saga turned Los Santos into a satirical, endlessly replayable playground — and it still sells like it came out yesterday.

Exhibit№ 04

Elden Ring

2022 · FromSoftware · Action RPG

FromSoftware opened its brutal, exacting combat into a vast open world, co-built with fantasy author George R. R. Martin, and made getting lost feel like the whole point.

Every Canon Invites an Argument.

This one is curated, not crowdsourced — forty-one years of the medium's history compressed into twenty verdicts and a great deal of internal debate. Yours would look different. That's rather the point of a canon: it's meant to be fought over, not just agreed with.

Built with an unreasonable amount of attention to brass corners. Rankings reflect the studio's own curation — your own top twenty is, as ever, the correct one.
